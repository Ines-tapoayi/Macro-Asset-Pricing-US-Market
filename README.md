# Macro-Asset-Pricing-US-Market
## Overview
This project studies the valuation of the US equity market between 1983 and 2025 using:
- the FED Model
- the Dividend Discount Model (DDM)
- Arrow-Pratt risk aversion recalibration

The objective is to analyze how monetary policy and interest rates affect equity market valuation.

## Objectives
- Estimate the implied equity risk premium (ERP)
- Study the relationship between equity prices, earnings, and long-term rates
- Estimate equilibrium interest rates
- Recalibrate the FED Model after 2008
- Diagnose US market valuation in 2025

## Methodology
### Models
- FED Model
- Dividend Discount Model (DDM)
- Arrow-Pratt risk aversion framework

### Statistical Analysis
- Linear regressions
- Historical ERP estimation
- Long-run equilibrium rate estimation
- Volatility-based recalibration

## Key Findings
### Before 2008
- FED Model remained relatively stable
- Strong sensitivity of valuations to long-term interest rates

### After 2008
- Decoupling between equity markets and interest rates
- Persistent low interest rates distorted valuation signals
- Higher market volatility justified a higher equilibrium ERP

### Final Diagnosis (2025)
After recalibration:
- US equity market appears broadly fairly valued
- Rising interest rates after 2022 partially restored FED Model consistency
